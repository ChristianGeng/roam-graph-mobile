#+title: Roam Graph help
#+STARTUP: showall

Roam Graph shows my org-roam notes on the phone and the tablet, without
Emacs. Syncthing copies the notes from the laptop; the app reads the folder,
builds the same graph org-roam builds, and draws it.

The app writes only new notes
([[id:roam-graph-help-capture][Capture and shared text]]); existing notes
are never changed.

These help pages are Org files with their own IDs, like notes. Tapping a
link opens the page, and Back returns, as in the node view. They are never
part of my graph, my search or what is shared with an AI.
