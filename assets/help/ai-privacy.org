#+title: AI privacy: hidden notes and web access
#+STARTUP: showall

What the AI can see, and what leaves the phone. The chat is described on
[[id:roam-graph-help-ai][the AI page]].

* Hidden from AI
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       roam-graph-help-hidden-from-ai
:END:
Notes with a tag such as ~:private:~ are never shown to an AI: not in a
share, not to the chat, not for an image or an edit. Headings with such a
tag are left out of the notes that are.

To set the tags:
1. Open AI settings (*Settings*, *AI*, or the gear on the first screen).
2. Open *Privacy*. In *Hidden from AI: tags*, tap the graph's tags below the field, or type
   a tag, also one no note has yet, and add it with space, comma, colon or
   Enter.
3. Go back to the AI settings list and tap the check mark to save.

A note that is hidden can still be deleted by hand, see
[[id:roam-graph-help-delete-note][Delete a note]].

* What leaves the phone
- The chat sends my messages and the notes its tools read to the chat
  provider, only when I send a message. Each provider's note in AI settings (AI → Chat)
  says where the data goes; some keep it in the EU. "EU only" limits some
  providers to their EU processing.
- A *voice memo* (a shared or dropped audio file) is sent whole to the
  provider of AI → Speech to text, Mistral (EU) or OpenAI (outside the EU),
  only after I agree the first time for that provider and only for the file
  I shared. The provider gets the recording itself; the text comes back to
  the capture screen and nothing is saved before I save.
- *Create image* sends the description and, if ticked, the note's title to
  the image provider.
- In agent mode, an image or infographic asked for in the chat is made from
  facts the AI read in the notes: they go into the image prompt, which is
  sent to the provider of *Images with:* or *Infographics with:*. These can
  differ from the chat provider and have their own keys. Notes hidden from
  AI stay out.
- *Edit with AI* sends the note, and its linked notes if chosen, to the
  chat provider.
- Keys are kept in the device's secure storage, never in the notes.

* Web access
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       roam-graph-help-web-access
:END:
With *Web access for the AI* on (AI settings → Web access; off by
default), the chat can also use the web, for questions the notes do not
answer:
- *fetch_web_page* reads a web page, with every provider. The page is
  fetched from the phone: only ~https://~ addresses, never an address in
  the own network (the phone itself, the router, other devices at home;
  also not after a redirect), without cookies or logins, at most 20
  seconds and about 100,000 characters of text.
- *web_search* is Anthropic's own search: the search words go to
  Anthropic, which searches and reads the results. Mistral has a web
  search only in another API than the one the chat uses, and Claude on
  Amazon Bedrock has none; there the AI only reads pages whose address it
  knows.

Searches and pages together are at most *Most web searches for one
message* (5 by default). The AI names its sources as links, also in the
notes it writes from facts of the web. Notes hidden from AI stay hidden:
the AI does not see them, so it cannot put them into a search.
