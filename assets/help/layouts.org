#+title: Layouts: circular, force-directed and radial
#+STARTUP: showall

A layout decides where the dots go. The links are the same in all; only
the picture changes. Switch in the graph's "View" menu (the chart icon),
under "Layout".

* Circular (circo)
Puts the nodes on circles, as Graphviz's ~circo~ does, the layout
~org-roam-graph~ uses. Nodes that link to each other share a circle, and
the circles are joined where they link. It is tidy and keeps its shape when
a note is added, so it is good for finding your way around a known graph.

* Force-directed
Treats links as springs and nodes as charges that push each other apart.
Linked notes pull together; unlinked ones drift away. Clusters of notes on
one topic show as clumps, and notes that join two topics sit between them.
It is good for seeing the shape of the graph: topics, bridges and loners.

* Radial
Puts the selected note in the middle, and the other notes on rings around
it: one link away on the first ring, two links away on the second, and so
on, links taken in both directions. A note sits near the note it is
reached from on the ring inside, and the notes reached from one note stay
together, so few links cross. Notes that cannot be reached from the
middle are drawn beside the rings.

To put another note in the middle, tap it, come back to the graph, and
choose "Centre" in the "More" menu: the rings are drawn again around it.
"Show in graph" from a note does the same. It is good for looking at one
note and its neighbourhood: how far away the other notes are.

* Which one
- To find a note, or to compare with ~org-roam-graph~ on the laptop:
  circular.
- To see topics and how they connect: force-directed, together with the
  [[id:roam-graph-help-views][Communities]] view.
- To see what is near one note: radial.

* At start
The graph opens with the force-directed layout. Settings, "Graph layout
at start", can choose circular or radial instead, or the layout chosen
last in the "View" menu ([[id:roam-graph-help-settings][Settings]]).
