#+title: Layouts: circular, force-directed, radial, grouped, hierarchical
#+STARTUP: showall

A layout decides where the dots go. The links are the same in all; only
the picture changes. Switch in the graph's "View" menu (the chart icon),
under "Layout".

* Circular (circo)
Puts the nodes on circles, as Graphviz's ~circo~ does, the layout
~org-roam-graph~ uses. Nodes that link to each other share a circle, and
the circles are joined where they link. It is tidy and keeps its shape when
a note is added, so it is good for finding your way around a known graph.

* Force-directed
Treats links as springs and nodes as charges that push each other apart.
Linked notes pull together; unlinked ones drift away. Clusters of notes on
one topic show as clumps, and notes that join two topics sit between them.
It is good for seeing the shape of the graph: topics, bridges and loners.

* Radial
Puts the selected note in the middle, and the other notes on rings around
it: one link away on the first ring, two links away on the second, and so
on, links taken in both directions. A note sits near the note it is
reached from on the ring inside, and the notes reached from one note stay
together, so few links cross. Notes that cannot be reached from the
middle are drawn beside the rings.

To put another note in the middle, tap it, come back to the graph, and
choose "Centre" in the "More" menu: the rings are drawn again around it.
"Show in graph" from a note does the same. It is good for looking at one
note and its neighbourhood: how far away the other notes are.

* Grouped by communities
Gives each topic of the notes its own area. The topics are the
communities that the [[id:roam-graph-help-views][Communities]] view colours, so with both on, each
area has one colour. Inside an area the notes are laid out
force-directed; the areas are side by side, the largest first, with free
space between them. Communities of one or two notes, and notes without
links, share one area at the end. A link between two areas is a note that
links across topics.

* Hierarchical (dot)
Puts the notes in layers from top to bottom, as Graphviz's ~dot~ draws:
a link points down, from the note that links to the note it links to. A
note sits one layer below the lowest note that links to it, so the notes
that nothing links to are at the top. Within a layer the notes are
ordered so that few links cross, and each note is near the middle of the
notes it links with. Two notes that link to each other count as one
link; where links go round in a circle, one of them is turned to point
up. Notes that are not connected are drawn beside each other.

It is good for a hub note and its sub-topics, or the part of the graph
around one note. On the whole graph, with its many cross links, the
layers get wide. When a graph is too large to lay out in a few seconds,
the force-directed layout is drawn instead, and a message says so.

* Which one
- To find a note, or to compare with ~org-roam-graph~ on the laptop:
  circular.
- To see topics and how they connect: force-directed, together with the
  [[id:roam-graph-help-views][Communities]] view.
- To see what is near one note: radial.
- To see the topics as places, and the notes that link them: grouped by
  communities.
- To see a hub and what hangs from it, top to bottom: hierarchical,
  with "Show" set to a part of the graph.

* At start
The graph opens with the force-directed layout. Settings, "Graph layout
at start", can choose any of the others instead, or the layout chosen
last in the "View" menu ([[id:roam-graph-help-settings][Settings]]).
