#+title: Settings
#+STARTUP: showall

*Settings* is in the graph's *More* menu. The first screen is short. From top
to bottom:

- *AI*: opens AI settings, see below. Under the name it shows the chat
  provider and model, for example "Mistral · mistral-large-4".
- *View*: opens the page *View*. Under the name it shows the theme and the
  graph layout at start, for example "System · Radial".
- *Note files*: a heading with two rows:
  - *Folder for new images*: where images made by the AI go, relative to
    ~roam/~ (~img~ by default). *Default* sets it back.
  - *Emacs command* (desktop only): what *Open in Emacs* runs; see
    [[id:roam-graph-help-desktop][The desktop app]]. On Android the row is
    not shown.

* The page View
From top to bottom:

- *Theme*: *System* (as Android is set), *Light* or *Dark*.
- *Sections when a note opens*: *Collapsed* (the top headings only),
  *Headings* (all headings, no text), or *Expanded* (everything). A note's
  own ~#+STARTUP:~ line comes first.
- *Graph layout at start*: *Force-directed*, *Circular (circo)*, *Radial*,
  *Grouped by communities*, *Hierarchical (dot)*, or *Last used*, the
  layout chosen last in the graph's *View* menu. The *View* menu still
  switches the layout while the graph is open; see
  [[id:roam-graph-help-layouts][Layouts]].
- *Graph width on the tablet*: shows the share of the graph next to a note.
  Drag the divider between them to change it; *Reset* goes back to 40 : 60.

* AI settings
*AI settings* is a short list of six rows. Each opens a page and shows its
current choice under the name. From top to bottom:
- *Chat*: the provider, with its base URL, *Model*, *Strong model
  (optional)* and key. The list icon (*Choose model*) fetches the
  provider's models, with the chips *Recommended* and *Calls tools* and an
  order by *Model ID* or *Price (lowest first)*; see
  [[id:roam-graph-help-models][Choosing a model]]. Some providers have
  *EU only*. Under the name: "Mistral · mistral-large-4".
- *Images*: *Images with:* (the chat's provider by default, or another with
  its own key, image model and "EU only"; see
  [[id:roam-graph-help-make-image][Make an image]]) and *Infographics
  with:* (see [[id:roam-graph-help-make-infographic][Make an
  infographic]]). Under the name: "Mistral · Vercel AI Gateway
  (infographics)".
- *Agent and limits*: *Let the AI change notes*, *Most changes the agent
  may make for one message*, *Most images the agent may make for one
  message*, *Most chats working at the same time*, *Notify when an answer is
  ready*, *Most notes one AI change set may edit*, *Most tool rounds per message* and *Most tokens per answer,
  thinking included*; see [[id:roam-graph-help-ai-edit][Let the AI edit
  notes]], [[id:roam-graph-help-parallel-chats][Run several chats]] and
  [[id:roam-graph-help-chat-notifications][Work in the background and
  notifications]]. Under the name:
  "Off", "Review" or "Agent".
- *Web access*: *Web access for the AI* (off by default) and *Most web
  searches for one message*; see
  [[id:roam-graph-help-web-access][Web access]]. Under the name: "Off", or
  "On, at most 5 searches".
- *Notes*: *Language of new notes*; see
  [[id:roam-graph-help-note-language][The language of notes]]. Under the
  name: "English (en)".
- *Privacy*: *Hidden from AI: tags*, the tags that hide notes from AI; see
  [[id:roam-graph-help-hidden-from-ai][Hidden from AI]]. Also *Keep chats
  for*; see [[id:roam-graph-help-continue-chat][Continue an earlier chat]]. Under the name:
  the tags, or "No tags".

All six pages change one draft. Nothing is saved until I tap the check mark
in the bar of the AI settings list; keys are then kept in the device's
secure storage, never in the notes. While something is not saved, the list
says "Unsaved changes". Going back from the list with unsaved changes asks
"Discard changes?".
