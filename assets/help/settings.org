#+title: Settings
#+STARTUP: showall

"Settings" is in the graph's "More" menu.

- *Sections when a note opens*: collapsed (the top headings only),
  headings (all headings, no text), or expanded (everything). A note's own
  ~#+STARTUP:~ line comes first.
- *AI*: the tags that hide notes from AI, and the chat's provider, base
  URL, models and key, with the limits of a chat; under *Images with:*,
  the provider for "Create image" (the chat's by default, or another with
  its own key, image model and "EU only"); under *Web*, *Web access
  for the AI* (off by default: search words go to the provider, pages
  are fetched from the phone) and the most web searches for one message.
  Keys are kept in
  Android's secure storage, never in the notes. See
  [[id:roam-graph-help-ai][AI]].
