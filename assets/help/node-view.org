#+title: The node view
#+STARTUP: showall

Shows a note as formatted Org: a whole file, or a heading with its subtree.
The sections below are tasks; the first one is about reading.

* Open a note and follow links
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       roam-graph-help-open-notes
:END:
1. In the graph, tap a node to open it (see
   [[id:roam-graph-help-graph-view][The graph view]]), or find it with
   *Search* (see [[id:roam-graph-help-search][Search and selected notes]]).
2. Tap an ~id:~ link to open the linked note on a new page. Back returns.
   A web, mail or phone link (~https:~, ~mailto:~, ~tel:~) opens in the app
   the device has for it, the browser on the desktop; a message names the
   address and has *Copy*. Other links, such as ~file:~, are shown but not
   opened.
3. Under the text, *Links* lists this note's ~id:~ links and *Backlinks*
   lists every link to this note, with where in the linking note it is.
   Tap an entry to open that note.
4. Tap *Show in graph* (the hub icon in the bar) to return to the graph,
   centred on this note.

Other things the page shows:
- *Sections* open collapsed or expanded, as set in
  [[id:roam-graph-help-settings][Settings]]; a note's own ~#+STARTUP:~
  line comes first. Tap a heading to open or close it.
- *Images* from the notes folder show at their Org width; tap one to zoom.
  SVG is drawn too. Web images stay links: the app reads only the folder.
- *LaTeX* formulas are rendered; ~\[ \]~ and ~$$ $$~ on their own line.
- *Mermaid* blocks show the image Emacs made; a block without one is drawn
  on the device, marked as such.
- *Source blocks* are coloured as Emacs colours them.
- Changes made in Emacs show within about 30 seconds while the app is
  open, once Syncthing has brought them.

* Search inside a note
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       roam-graph-help-search-in-note
:END:
1. Tap *Search in this note* (the page icon in the bar), or press Ctrl+F
   (Cmd+F on macOS) on the desktop.
2. Type the text. Every place it is in the note is marked, upper and lower
   case alike, and the sections they are in open.
3. The bar counts the matches. The arrows go to the next or the previous
   one, around the ends; Enter goes to the next.
4. Tick *Only matching sections* to fold away the sections without a match,
   as Org's sparse tree does.
5. Close the search with the x, Back or Escape. The marks go and the
   sections are folded as they were.

To search all notes instead, use *Search* in the bar; see
[[id:roam-graph-help-search][Search and selected notes]].

* Tick a TODO or a checkbox, and undo
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       roam-graph-help-todo
:END:
These quick edits are saved at once, without the diff; a short message
confirms the change, and closes after about two seconds.
- *Checkboxes*: tap a list item with a checkbox to tick or untick it, as
  Org's C-c C-c does; ~[-]~ becomes ~[X]~. The boxes and progress cookies
  (~[1/3]~, ~[33%]~) above it follow.
- *TODO states*: slide a heading to the left for its next state, or
  long-press it for the list of states: the note's ~#+TODO:~ keywords, or
  TODO and DONE. With ~#+STARTUP: logdone~, a heading that becomes done
  gets a ~CLOSED:~ time, and loses it when it is no longer done. If the note
  changed in Emacs since it was shown, nothing is saved and the note is
  shown again.

To undo, tap *Undo the last change* (the undo arrow in the bar). Each tap
undoes one quick edit, newest first, for as long as you stay in this note.
The arrow disappears when no steps are left, when you leave the note (Back,
another note or *Show in graph*), when you open any surface in the *Edit*
menu, or when the file changes from outside. Returning to the note does not
bring the steps back.

* Edit a note
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       roam-graph-help-edit-note
:END:
The *Edit* menu (the ⋮ icon in the bar) holds these. Each writes only
after I confirm it (*Save*, *Delete*, *Add to
the note* or *Restore*), and the old version is kept (*Earlier versions*).
- *Edit text*: edit the note's text. Tap *Review* to see the change, then
  *Save*. Nothing is saved before that.
- *Add to this node*: type Org text, and tap *Save*; it goes to the end of
  the file, or of the heading after its subheadings. Nothing there changes.
- *Title, tags and aliases* changes the note's head. The tags are chips; tap
  the x to remove one. Below, the tags of the graph, the most used first
  with their number of notes, can be tapped. Typing filters them; space,
  comma, colon or Enter adds what is typed, and a tag no note has yet shows
  as *New tag*. Only letters, digits and ~_@#%~ can be in a tag.
- *Edit with AI*: the AI changes this note, and if asked its linked notes,
  after a diff; see [[id:roam-graph-help-ai-edit][Let the AI edit notes]].
- *Create image*: see [[id:roam-graph-help-make-image][Make an image]].
- *Earlier versions*: the old texts the app kept, to bring one back.
- *Delete note…*: see
  [[id:roam-graph-help-delete-note][Delete a note]].

** In Edit text
- *Insert link* puts a link to a note you choose at the cursor; selected
  text becomes the link's description.
- The buttons below the text act on the line of the cursor, or on the
  selected lines, as Org's commands do: *New heading* (after the current
  subtree, at its level), *Up a level* and *Down a level* (a heading with
  its subtree, a list item with its sub-items), *Move up* and *Move down*
  (past the heading or item before or after it at the same level), *New list
  item* (with the same bullet; numbers count on), *Checkbox on or off*, and
  *Next TODO state*. A heading above a property drawer keeps its level and
  its place among the drawers; such a button does nothing and says why.

** Links
- *+* adds a link: next to *Links*, to the note you choose, on this
  note's "See also:" line; next to *Backlinks*, to this note, on the
  chosen note's "See also:" line. Type a title that is no note's, and the
  list offers a *New note* with it first, as Emacs's org-roam-node-insert
  does; it is made at once and the link goes to it. The change shows
  first, and is saved as every edit.
- *Trash icons* remove a link: on a link, from this note; on a backlink,
  from the other note. The link becomes its text, so nothing is lost. On an
  image, the image link goes with its ~#+caption:~ and ~# image prompt:~
  lines; the image file stays in the folder. Each shows the change first,
  and is saved as every edit.

* Delete a note
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       roam-graph-help-delete-note
:END:
1. In the *Edit* menu (⋮), choose *Delete note…*. The same action is in
   the graph (long press on a node; see
   [[id:roam-graph-help-graph-view][The graph view]]) and in search, for
   several selected notes (see
   [[id:roam-graph-help-search][Search and selected notes]]).
2. The dialog names the file or subtree that will go. For a file it counts
   the heading nodes in it and the notes that link to it.
3. Choose what happens to links to the note: *Turn the links into plain
   text* is the default; *Leave the links* keeps dead links, as deleting in
   Emacs does.
4. Tap *Delete*. *Cancel* writes nothing.

After the deletion, the previous note or the graph is shown. The message's
*Undo* restores the deletion and the link changes as one step. It stays
until the next action or a swipe. Later, use *Recent changes* in the
graph's *More* menu. An undo never overwrites newer text; notes left
unchanged are named.

Only files directly in ~roam/~ can be deleted here, including notes hidden
from AI.

* Share a note
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       roam-graph-help-share-note
:END:
*Share note…* in the ⋮ menu (or in *More for this note* when the note
cannot be edited) opens Android's share menu, for example to send the note
to a Telegram chat. It asks how:
- *As text*: the title, then the text of the note without its property
  drawers, IDs, ~#+~ lines and comments. Links show their description.
  Headings, lists and tables stay as they are. A caption stays as text.
- *As text with images*: the same text and the image files of the note.
- *As Org file*: the ~.org~ file, named after the note. For a heading node
  it holds the heading and what is below it.

A note hidden from AI can be shared too: this is your own action. On the
desktop, *As text* copies the text ("Copied") and files are saved; see
[[id:roam-graph-help-desktop][The desktop app]]. To share one picture, see
[[id:roam-graph-help-share-picture][Share a picture]].

* Other buttons in the bar
- *Open in Orgro* hands the file to Orgro, to read or edit it there; on the
  desktop it is *Open in Emacs*, see
  [[id:roam-graph-help-desktop][The desktop app]].
- *New node from here* writes a new note linking to this one; see
  [[id:roam-graph-help-capture][Capture]].
- On a narrow screen, *New node from here* and *Open in Orgro* are in
  *More for this note* (…).
- The *AI* menu has *Ask AI* and *Share with AI*; see
  [[id:roam-graph-help-ai][AI]].
