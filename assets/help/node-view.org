#+title: The node view
#+STARTUP: showall

Shows a note as formatted Org: a whole file, or a heading with its subtree.

- *Links*: an ~id:~ link opens the linked note on a new page; Back
  returns. Other links are shown but not opened.
- *Search in this note* (the page icon in the bar, or Ctrl+F on the
  desktop) marks every place the typed text is in the note, upper and
  lower case alike, and opens the sections they are in. The bar counts
  them; the arrows go to the next or the previous one, around the ends,
  and Enter goes to the next. *Only matching sections* folds away the
  sections without a match, as Org's sparse tree does. Closing the
  search (the x, Back or Escape) removes the marks and folds the
  sections as they were. On a narrow screen, *New node from here* and
  *Open in Orgro* are in the *More* menu.
- *Links* and *Backlinks*: below the text, this note's ~id:~ links, and
  every link to this note, with where in the linking note it is.
- *Trash icons* remove a link: on a link, from this note; on a backlink,
  from the other note. The link becomes its text, so nothing is lost. On an
  image, the image link goes with its ~#+caption:~ and ~# image prompt:~
  lines; the image file stays in the folder. Each shows the change first,
  and is saved as every edit, with the old version kept.
- *+* adds a link: next to *Links*, to the note you choose, on this
  note's "See also:" line; next to *Backlinks*, to this note, on the
  chosen note's "See also:" line. Type a title that is no note's, and the
  list offers a *New note* with it first, as Emacs's org-roam-node-insert
  does; it is made at once and the link goes to it. The change shows
  first, and is saved as every edit.
- *Title, tags and aliases* in the edit menu changes the note's head. The
  tags are chips; tap the x to remove one. Below, the tags of the graph,
  the most used first with their number of notes, can be tapped. Typing
  filters them; space, comma, colon or Enter adds what is typed, and a tag
  no note has yet shows as *New tag*. Only letters, digits and ~_@#%~ can
  be in a tag.
- *Checkboxes*: tap a list item with a checkbox to tick or untick it, as
  Org's C-c C-c does; ~[-]~ becomes ~[X]~. The boxes and progress cookies
  (~[1/3]~, ~[33%]~) above it follow. It is saved at once, without the
  diff; the message has *Undo*.
- *TODO states*: slide a heading to the left for its next state, or
  long-press it for the list of states: the note's ~#+TODO:~ keywords, or
  TODO and DONE. With ~#+STARTUP: logdone~, a heading that becomes done
  gets a ~CLOSED:~ time, and loses it when it is no longer done. Saved at
  once, with *Undo*. If the note changed in Emacs since it was shown,
  nothing is saved and the note is shown again.
- *Insert link* in *Edit text* puts a link to a note you choose at the
  cursor; selected text becomes the link's description.
- The buttons below the text in *Edit text* act on the line of the
  cursor, or on the selected lines, as Org's commands do: *New heading*
  (after the current subtree, at its level), *Up a level* and *Down a
  level* (a heading with its subtree, a list item with its sub-items),
  *Move up* and *Move down* (past the heading or item before or after it
  at the same level), *New list item* (with the same bullet; numbers
  count on), *Checkbox on or off*, and *Next TODO state*. A heading above
  a property drawer keeps its level and its place among the drawers;
  such a button does nothing and says why. Nothing is saved before
  *Review* and *Save*.
- Changes made in Emacs show within about 30 seconds while the app is
  open, once Syncthing has brought them.
- *Sections* open collapsed or expanded, as set in
  [[id:roam-graph-help-settings][Settings]]; a note's own ~#+STARTUP:~
  line comes first. Tap a heading to open or close it.
- *Images* from the notes folder show at their Org width; tap one to zoom.
  SVG is drawn too. Web images stay links: the app reads only the folder.
- *LaTeX* formulas are rendered; ~\[ \]~ and ~$$ $$~ on their own line.
- *Mermaid* blocks show the image Emacs made; a block without one is drawn
  on the device, marked as such.
- *Source blocks* are coloured as Emacs colours them.
- *Open in Orgro* hands the file to Orgro, to read or edit it there.
- *Show in graph* returns to the graph, centred on this note.
- *New node from here* writes a new note linking to this one; see
  [[id:roam-graph-help-capture][Capture]].
- The *AI* menu: see [[id:roam-graph-help-ai][AI]].
